# Why Use ChatBot Builder?

Most chatbot tools are either locked behind paywalls, require backend servers, or force you into rigid templates. **ChatBot Builder** is different:

## 🔑 Key Reasons
- **No backend required** — everything runs in the browser.
- **Instant export** — generate a single `.js` file you can drop into any site.
- **Rule-driven simplicity** — start with plain text rules, or go advanced with JavaScript.
- **Accessible** — free, open, and source-available.
- **Remixable** — add or remove features, customize logic, and share your own versions.

## 🌐 Who is it for?
- Developers who want a lightweight chatbot without server setup.
- Educators teaching rule-based logic.
- Hobbyists experimenting with conversational bots.
- Businesses needing quick FAQ bots without infrastructure.

## 🚀 Vision
ChatBot Builder is designed to be **modular, playful, and empowering**. It’s not just a demo — it’s a real app you can use, remix, and deploy anywhere.
